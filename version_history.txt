This is the document about the evolution of the game

Pretext

The theme of this game is underground, more specifically in a cave. You play as a robot who can move around.


Before Mon 17 Aug:
 - Game had 1 level
 - Player was the only entity
 - 2 kinds of ores
 - The ability to pick up ores
 - y sort, camera zoom and window resizing


Mon 17 Aug:
 - Decided to determine the player's purpose in this game which is to complete as many levels as possible, with the hope that that is fun for the player as well
 - Each level will be a new map (most likely manually created) and to beat the level you must find gems/ores/other things for the gatekeeper, who will only let you out of the cave if he gets those materials from you.
 - Sometimes, something you will have to find will be hidden behind a door which needs a secret key. The key will be hidden elsewhere in the cave, and it is your job to find it.
 - To make finding the key easier, you can talk to NPCs or other entities who can give you clues/riddles to the whereabouts of keys.
 - As the levels go up, the difficulty of the levels increases as well and this can be done by:
    a) Reducing the visible part of the screen to a spotlight around the player to limit the players view of their surroundings. The spotlight radius can be increased by the player throughout the course of the level
    b) Including more things that the player has to find for the gatekeeper.
    c) ...
